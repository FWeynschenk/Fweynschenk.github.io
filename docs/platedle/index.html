How it works

Every day you get one roll: a completely random numberplate from one of 35+ countries, each with its real format and look. Dutch plates are the daily bread; a German city code, a US state plate or — if the gods smile — a Vatican plate gets rarer and rarer. Every plate is scanned for over 60 patterns — palindromes, primes, meme numbers, words, Fibonacci numbers, vintage Dutch sidecodes and more — and every pattern found earns a badge, as does the country (and US state) itself.

Badges award EP (entropy points) based on how unlikely they are: Common (>10%) is worth 250 EP, Uncommon 1,000, Rare 5,000, Epic 25,000, Legendary 100,000 and Mythic (<0.001%) a monstrous 500,000 EP.

Your total EP is compared against the universe of all possible plates to grade your roll, from Trash (bottom 1%) all the way up to Mythic (top 1%). Come back daily to keep your streak alive and complete the badge collection.
