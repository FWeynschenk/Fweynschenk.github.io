How it works

Every day you get one roll: a completely random Dutch-style numberplate. Older plate formats (sidecodes) are rarer, just like on the road. Your plate is scanned for patterns — palindromes, primes, meme numbers, words, vintage formats and more — and every pattern found earns a badge.

Badges award EP (entropy points) based on how unlikely they are: Common (>10%) is worth 1 EP, Uncommon 5, Rare 25, Epic 100, Legendary 500 and Mythic (<0.001%) a whopping 2500 EP.

Your total EP is compared against the universe of all possible plates to grade your roll, from Trash (bottom 1%) all the way up to Mythic (top 1%). Come back daily to keep your streak alive and complete the badge collection.
